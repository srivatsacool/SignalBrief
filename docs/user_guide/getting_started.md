# SignalBrief User Guide & Quickstart

SignalBrief delivers curated intelligence reports to your inbox every morning.

## For Subscribers

1. **Daily Intelligence Brief**:
   - Arrives at your configured delivery time in your local timezone.
   - Contains 3 to 5 core developments in your selected domain (e.g. Manufacturing).
   - Each development details:
     - **What changed**: The core factual update.
     - **Why it matters**: Strategic impact on operations, technology, or supply.
     - **What to watch**: Forward-looking triggers or pending milestones.
2. **Dashboard Exploration**:
   - Access the web dashboard to search historical briefs.
   - Browse by topic cluster or entity.
   - Customize domain keywords or pause delivery when away.
